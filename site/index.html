How it works

Headlines first, slides second, lint last

01 · spine

Write the case as claims

Every headline is a full sentence with a verb. Read top to bottom, the list alone should make your case.

02 · build

One job per slide

A number, a chart, a screenshot, or three short points. One HTML file with speaker notes and print to PDF.

03 · lint

Check every word

Filler, topic-label headlines, crowded slides and numbers without a source get flagged before anyone sees them.

Try it

Paste your slides and see what a judge will notice

First line of each slide is the headline. Separate slides with a line of three dashes. Add "source:" under any number. Nothing leaves your browser.

    The rules

    Eight rules the skill will not break

    A headline is a claim, never a topic label.

    Twelve words or fewer per headline.

    One piece of evidence per slide.

    Every number carries its source.

    One accent color, used where the eye should land.

    No emoji, no exclamation marks, no gradient text.

    Missing data is marked on the slide, never invented.

    Under three minutes for a hackathon pitch.
