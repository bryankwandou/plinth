## Removed from the original deck

- **47,94% petani miskin ekstrem**: not on the UGM page it cites, and the BPS release it links reports national poverty (8,47%), not farmers. Removed until a BPS table is found.
- **571T / 42,7T / 10T market**: no source on the slide; two rows carry the same label with different numbers. Needs OJK or Kemenko Perekonomian KUR data.
- **"Belum ada investasi sektor pangan yang tergolong aman"**: an opinion, not data.

## Cuts against the pitch (say it before a judge does)

- N2: pests beat weather as the top problem. BMKG weather-index insurance does not pay for pests. Answer: combine with Jasindo AUTP, which covers pests.

- N5 is the guarantor's own product page, not the regulation. NusaHarvest has no agreement with Askrindo or Jamkrindo; the deck says it plans to route loans through KUR-eligible cooperatives, nothing more.
- N4 is a national count of smallholders, not the reachable market; the pilot is one cooperative.
