## Notes

- Against the pitch (C7, C8): a well-funded bookkeeping app for exactly this user closed within about 16 months of a Rp 1,14 triliun round. The deck names it on its own slide and answers it (no app to install, no separate habit to form).
- Also against: C1 shows literacy rising (65,43% in 2024); "warung owners can't count" is not a safe claim. The deck argues about a missing habit, not about ability.
- C4 is dated (2019-era count repeated in 2021-2022 releases). A newer official count was not found in this session.
- C6 is a vendor survey, sample size unknown. Say so if a judge asks.
- Findex Indonesia country-level account ownership could not be fetched as static text (World Bank data portal renders by script); only the SECONDARY eight-country figure is used, in notes only.
- The market arithmetic on slide 8 (1% of C5 merchants x Rp15.000/month) is our assumption, not outside data, and is labelled as such.
